Our Story & Heritage

The Story Behind ElleBee

Created for women who cherish the quiet confidence of intentional handcrafted adornments.

Philosophy & Craft

Beads as Adornment, Beads as Identity

ElleBeeLuxeBeads was born from a deep love of color, culture, and feminine elegance. What began as a personal passion for creating dainty jewelry pieces has grown into a cherished boutique brand dedicated to high-vibrational beauty.

Every strand is hand-selected and carefully strung with intention in Lagos, Nigeria. Whether it is our signature waist beads that accentuate your natural silhouette or our layered bridal pearl chokers, we take immense pride in crafting pieces that make you feel radiant and empowered.

We celebrate the beauty of custom jewelry — where your preferred colors, measurements, and stones come together seamlessly through direct, friendly conversations on WhatsApp.
